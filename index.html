Why work with Davenport Books

Publishing a book and telling readers about it are two big jobs. We help with both, in a way that stays organized and easy to follow.

  • One place for publishing and marketing support

    Formatting, covers, listings, ads, and launch planning can be handled together, so you don't have to coordinate a different provider for every step.

  • Professional presentation

    Your book, your listings, and your promotional materials are prepared to look consistent and credible to readers.

  • An author-focused approach

    We start with your book, your audience, and your goals, then suggest the services that fit them.

  • A clear, organized process

    You'll know what stage your project is in, what happens next, and what we need from you.

  • Flexible services that grow with you

    Start with one service, such as formatting, and add marketing support later as your needs change.

Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Send it through the contact form and we'll get back to you.

What services does Davenport Books offer?

We offer publishing and book services (KDP publishing assistance, book formatting, book cover design, Amazon book optimization, and Goodreads optimization) and marketing services (ads management, social media marketing, author email marketing, and book launch campaign management).

Do you work with self-published authors?

Yes. Davenport Books is built for self-published authors and independent publishers who want professional support without managing every task themselves.

Can I hire only one service?

Yes. Every service can be requested on its own. Many authors start with one need, such as formatting or a cover, and add other services later.

Can you help with Amazon KDP?

Yes. KDP Publishing Assistance covers preparing your book and listing details for Kindle Direct Publishing. We can also help improve your Amazon book page through Amazon Book Optimization.

Can you help with book launches?

Yes. Book Launch Campaign Management helps coordinate the timeline, materials, and promotional activities around your release. Results depend on many factors, and we don't promise specific sales or reviews.

Do you provide book cover design?

Yes. Book Cover Design is one of our publishing services. Tell us about your book, genre, and audience, and we'll discuss options with you.

How does the process work?

We follow four steps: Discover, Prepare, Launch, and Grow. First we learn about your book and goals, then we prepare your materials, coordinate the launch, and review how things are going so we can keep improving. See the Process section for details.

How can I get started?

Use the contact form below or email us directly. Tell us about your book, where you are in the process, and what kind of help you're looking for.
