Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Davenport Books is a publishing and book-marketing service operated as a division of Davenport Labs. This page explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies only to this website and not to any external platforms you may reach through links on this site.

Information We Collect

The only place this website collects personal information is the contact form in the “Work With Us” section. When you submit that form, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The service or services you selected
  • The content of the message you wrote

We do not collect any other information about you, and this website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking tools of any kind.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Discuss the services you are interested in
  • Correspond with you about a potential or ongoing project
  • Keep a record of our communication with you

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How Your Information Is Transmitted

This website does not store form submissions on its own server — there is no database or backend. When you press “Send message”, the form opens your own email application with your details already filled in, and the message is sent from your email account to jeffrey.davenport.000@gmail.com.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service involved in handling your information is Google (Gmail), which provides the email inbox that receives your message. Google’s own privacy policy applies to how they handle email data. We do not use any other third-party services for this website — no form processors, no analytics platforms, no advertising networks, and no payment processors.

Cookies and Analytics

This website does not use cookies, and it does not use any analytics or tracking tools. Nothing is stored in your browser by this site.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. Email correspondence is kept within the Gmail account referenced above, which uses standard email security features such as encryption in transit and account access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We keep email correspondence for as long as it is useful for responding to your enquiry or maintaining a client relationship. If you would like us to delete our record of your communication, you can ask us to do so (see “Your Rights” below).

Your Rights

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct that information
  • Ask us to delete it

Send your request to jeffrey.davenport.000@gmail.com, and we will respond as promptly as we can.

Children’s Privacy

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through the contact form, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we change how the website collects or uses information — for example, by adding a form-processing service or analytics — we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to review this page occasionally.

Contact Us
